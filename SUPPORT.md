# Alike Support

- **Help and contact:** [beyondthecode.app/alike/support](https://www.beyondthecode.app/alike/support) or [apps@beyondthecode.app](mailto:apps@beyondthecode.app)
- **Bug reports and ideas:** open an [issue](https://github.com/beyondthecode-bc/Alike/issues/new/choose). Please don't attach personal photos.
- **Translations:** every language Alike speaks is in [`languages/`](languages). Spot a wrong or awkward phrase? Open a [translation issue](https://github.com/beyondthecode-bc/Alike/issues/new?template=translation.md) with the English key and your suggestion.

## Common questions

- **Where did my photos go?** Removed items are in Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted for 30 days. Settings › Recovery window in Alike shows how many days are left.
- **Why is the size smaller than iPhone Storage shows for an app?** iPhone Storage counts the app's own private storage (for example WhatsApp chats). Alike can only see the copies an app saved to your Photos library.
- **Manage or cancel Alike Pro:** Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- **Restore a purchase on another device:** Settings › Alike Pro › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
