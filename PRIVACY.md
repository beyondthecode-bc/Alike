# Alike Privacy Policy

Alike is built so your photos stay yours.

- **No data collected.** Alike has no account, server, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or third-party SDK. The developer receives nothing about you or your library.
- **On-device analysis.** Photos and videos are analyzed on your iPhone, iPad or Mac with Apple frameworks. The index Alike keeps (fingerprints, groups and the 30-day recovery journal) stays in the app's own storage and is excluded from backups.
- **No network requests of its own.** Alike never uploads photos. When you choose to include iCloud items, Apple's Photos framework may load previews from your own iCloud library; that traffic goes only between your device and Apple.
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The developer never sees your payment details.
- **Deleting.** Nothing is removed until you confirm in the system dialog, and removed items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

The full policy is at [beyondthecode.app/alike/privacy](https://www.beyondthecode.app/alike/privacy). Questions: [apps@beyondthecode.app](mailto:apps@beyondthecode.app).
